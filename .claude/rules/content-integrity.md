# Content integrity rule

- Do not convert prototype text, memory, implication or stylistic filler into published fact.
- When adding or materially changing factual public content, consult `docs/CONTENT_SOURCES.md`.
- Prefer owner-confirmed or official primary-source information.
- If provenance is insufficient, preserve an explicit placeholder or flag the item as requiring confirmation.
- Keep assumptions visible in the PR/task summary instead of silently resolving them.
