# Content publication check

1. Read `docs/CONTENT_SOURCES.md` and identify every factual claim being added or changed.
2. Determine the strongest available provenance for each material claim.
3. Do not treat prototype copy, memory, inference or existing rendered text as verification by itself.
4. If provenance is sufficient, preserve the source meaning and avoid embellishment.
5. If provenance is insufficient, mark the item `REQUIRES CONFIRMATION` or keep an explicit placeholder; do not guess.
6. Summarize what is publishable and what remains unverified.
