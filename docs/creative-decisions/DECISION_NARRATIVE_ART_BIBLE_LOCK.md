# Decision — Narrative & Art Bible Product Owner Lock

**Decision ID:** `CDL-027`  
**Date:** 2026-09-16  
**Status:** `PRODUCT OWNER LOCKED`  
**Approved by:** Product Owner  
**Applies to:** `docs/NARRATIVE_ART_BIBLE.md` v0.4 creative direction

## Locked creative baseline

The Product Owner accepts the Narrative & Art Bible v0.4 as the governing creative baseline for In Cerca di Avventura, including:

- the **cittadella-crocevia** foundational metaphor;
- the principle that reality may be narrated as legend but never falsified;
- the factual/diegetic boundary and rule that fact/usability wins;
- the emotional direction: mysterious without confusion, ancient without ruin, solemn without pomposity, warm, inhabited and handcrafted;
- the material language: blackened/burnished iron, antique gold, midnight blue, restrained crimson, stone, fabric, parchment, ink and seals;
- selective atmospheric light rather than glow-heavy game UI;
- disciplined ornament and decreasing fantasy density as repetition/data density increases;
- one design system across multiple places, without page-specific mini-systems;
- editorial register ladder and restrained narrative microcopy;
- heraldic/iconographic rules preventing arbitrary symbolism;
- responsive art direction: preserve identity and hierarchy, reduce decorative density before reducing legibility or target size.

## Expressive-label boundary

This lock does **not** silently canonize every poetic label mentioned in the Bible.

Current status remains:

- `Gli Annali` — locked narrative identity for Cronache;
- `Il Libro dei Nomi` — candidate expressive identity for Avventurieri, non-blocking;
- `Il Campo delle Prove` — candidate expressive identity for Giostre, non-blocking;
- `I Reami Alleati` — optional/conditional page-level expression only where a Page Dossier confirms it;
- `I Proclami dell'Araldo` — optional/conditional narrative expression, not the mandatory functional destination name;
- Home poetic page title — optional and not locked;
- Home H08 closing headline — separately locked by `CDL-026` as **“Trova la tua prossima Adunanza.”** when the Adunanze route is available.

## Consequence

`docs/NARRATIVE_ART_BIBLE.md` may now be treated as `PRODUCT_OWNER_LOCKED_SCOPE` for its creative direction. Downstream canonical propagation may proceed, while unresolved/candidate page-level expressive labels retain their own review status.

This decision does not by itself authorize production artwork. Asset/UI production remains governed by the Creative Canonical Lock sequence and downstream reconciliation of the Canonical Spec, specialist documents, asset manifest and responsive shell evidence.
