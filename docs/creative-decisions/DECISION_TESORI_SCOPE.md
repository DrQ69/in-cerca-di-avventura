# ICA Creative Decision Note — Tesori scope

**Decision ID:** ICA-DEC-TER-001  
**Status:** SUSPENDED — direction recorded, not canonical  
**Date:** 2026-09-15  
**Authority:** Product Owner  
**Related documents:** `docs/TERMINOLOGY_BIBLE.md`, `docs/NARRATIVE_ART_BIBLE.md`

## Product Owner direction to preserve

The current working hypothesis that **Tesori** should be the umbrella for guides, tools, documents and printable resources is **not approved** and must not be treated as canonical.

The Product Owner sees **Tesori** more naturally as the collecting / rarity area of In Cerca di Avventura.

Candidate subject matter includes, without yet freezing taxonomy or page architecture:

- the most valuable and rare Sorcery cards;
- Curio cards and related information;
- Artist Proofs;
- original paintings / artworks;
- other rare, prestigious, unusual or collectible Sorcery material;
- additional collecting-oriented subjects to be defined later.

This direction is intentionally **recorded but suspended**. No final UX label hierarchy, technical object model, content taxonomy, launch scope or navigation placement is approved yet.

## Guides, documents and regulations — unresolved placement

Guides, documents, regulations, printable material and similar reference resources should **not automatically be assigned to Tesori**.

A possible future direction is to host them in a dedicated subsection within **Cronache**, but this is only a Product Owner hypothesis at this stage and remains unresolved.

Before implementation, the project must explicitly decide whether these materials belong:

- inside Cronache as a structured reference area;
- in another dedicated section;
- across multiple sections according to content type.

## Consequences for current documentation

Until this decision is resumed:

1. Any statement in the current Terminology Bible describing `Tesori` primarily as guides/tools/documents/resources is **RECHECK_REQUIRED** and must not be promoted to Canonical Spec or implementation.
2. `La Camera delle Reliquie` remains creative source material only; its final meaning is not frozen.
3. No `RESOURCE` entity, taxonomy, component or navigation item should be created solely on the basis of the earlier Tesori-as-resources hypothesis.
4. The collecting direction above must be reviewed when the Tesori Page Dossier is created.
5. The placement of guides/documents/regulations must be resolved independently during the Cronache / content-architecture work.

## Resume gate

This decision can move from `SUSPENDED` only after Product Owner review of:

- the exact collecting scope of Tesori;
- whether Curio, Artist Proofs, valuable/rare cards and original artworks are separate categories or facets;
- editorial vs catalogue/database treatment;
- data sources and provenance requirements;
- launch content readiness;
- relationship with Mercante, Cronache and card data;
- final narrative place name and UX label.
